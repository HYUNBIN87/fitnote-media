Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy describes how the "fitnote" application ("the App"), operated by 66 FitNote, handles information.

Who uses the App

The App is an internal tool used only by the operator of 66 FitNote to publish its own content to its own social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, Threads). It is not offered to the public.

Information we access

When the account owner authorizes the App, we receive an access token and basic account information (open ID, display name) for that account only. We do not access any other user's data.

How we use it

The access token is used solely to upload and publish content that the account owner has approved. It is stored on the operator's own computer and is never sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties.

Retention and deletion

The account owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app settings (Security > Manage app permissions). Tokens are deleted when access is revoked or the App is no longer used.
